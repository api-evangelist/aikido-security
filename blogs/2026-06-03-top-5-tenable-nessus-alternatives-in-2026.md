Tenable Nessus is a widely deployed vulnerability scanner with over 300,000 plugin checks and a 25-year track record in network and infrastructure security, but its limited built-in remediation workflows require manual export to ticketing systems. The article argues that powerful tools disconnected from developer workflows become ineffective, and compares five alternatives better suited to modern engineering teams: Aikido Security, Snyk, Checkmarx, Wiz, and Rapid7.
